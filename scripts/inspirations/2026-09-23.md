### Poem of the Day

> #### <span style="font-size: smaller;">Excerpt from</span><br>Joy in the Woods
> **Claude McKay**
> 
> But oh! for the woods, the flowers  
> &emsp;&emsp;Of natural, sweet perfume,  
> The heartening, summer showers  
> &emsp;&emsp;And the smiling shrubs in bloom,  
> Dust-free, dew-tinted at morn,  
> &emsp;&emsp;The fresh and life-giving air,  
> The billowing waves of corn  
> &emsp;&emsp;And the birds’ notes rich and clear:—
> 
> For a man-machine toil-tired  
> May crave beauty too—though he’s hired.
