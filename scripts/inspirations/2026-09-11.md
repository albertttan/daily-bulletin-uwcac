### Poeme of the Day

> #### Our world

> Planes fly high through the sky,
>
> dropping bombs as people die, 
>
> I sit in my bed asking why,
>
> millions of souls lost their lives,
>
> just for some money and status and power,
>
> all of our freedom the system devours,
>
> I get what I want regardless the cost,
>
> kids work to survive their sparkle is lost,
>
> I want the latest iPhone,
>
> a bomb destroyed their home,
>
> while I complain about the food,
>
> their last meal was postponed,
>
> young girls forced into motherhood, 
>
> yet I'm the one who feels misunderstood, 
>
> their freedom of speech is ripped away, 
>
> while I plan my next holiday.

**Written by Anonymous.**
